A job search that runs from your inbox

Every morning this agent pulls fresh postings from job boards, scores each one against what you told it you want, and emails you the ten best. You reply to that email with the numbers of the ones you like. Within fifteen minutes it emails back a cover letter for each, written from your resume, with a link to the application. You paste the letter in and submit.

Setup takes about five minutes and is the only time you type anything here. After that it runs from your inbox.

What it does

  • Searches the Remotive, Greenhouse and Lever job boards every morning, and Indeed when Indeed allows it.
  • Scores each posting from 0 to 100 against your criteria, with a one-line reason you can read in the email.
  • Emails the ten best. Anything else that scored well waits for tomorrow, so nothing is lost.
  • Writes one cover letter per job you pick, then checks it for the tells of machine writing and rewrites once if it finds any.
  • Keeps a dashboard of everything: new matches, the ones you picked, the ones you've applied to. You can edit any letter there before you use it.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't submit applications for you. You paste the letter and click submit. Every employer's form is different, and a form filled in wrong costs you the job.
  • It doesn't search LinkedIn. LinkedIn has no public job API and blocks scraping. Running one against your login would put your account at risk.
  • It doesn't act without you. Nothing is selected or written until you reply.
  • It doesn't email anyone but you. No employer hears from this app.
  • It doesn't share your data between users. Your jobs, resume and letters are yours alone. The person running this copy can, in principle, read the database, as with any app a friend hosts for you. Your AI key is the exception: it's stored encrypted, so even they can't read it.

About the AI key

Scoring hundreds of postings and writing letters uses an AI model, and AI models are paid for per use. Rather than one person paying for everyone, each account uses its own key, so the cost lands where the benefit does.

  • Which kind. Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. Paste whichever you have; the app can tell which from the key itself.
  • Don't have one? A Gemini key is free to create at aistudio.google.com/apikey and takes about a minute. Light use stays inside Google's free tier; heavier use costs cents a day.
  • How it's stored. Encrypted. It's shown back to you only as its first and last four characters, and you can replace or remove it in Settings at any time.
  • If you skip it. You can still finish signing up. Nothing can be scored until you add one in Settings, and your dashboard will say so.

Have ready

Your resume, as a PDF or text you can paste, and your AI key if you have one.
